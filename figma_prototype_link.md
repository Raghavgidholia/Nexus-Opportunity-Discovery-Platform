### 💡 Evaluator Guidance
* **Interactive Hotspots:** Click anywhere on the canvas to highlight clickable regions in blue if you get stuck on a user flow.
* **Key Flow to Test:** Pay special attention to the *Publish & Match Target Feeds* action sequence, which triggers the automated success confirmation modals.
* **Full-Screen Optimization:** For the best experience, click the **Options** dropdown in the top-right corner of the prototype window and select **"Fit to screen"**.
